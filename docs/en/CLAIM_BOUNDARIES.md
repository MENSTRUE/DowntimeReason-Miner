# Claims and interpretation

OK and DIFFICULT are logbook PROBLEM_TYPE annotations; neither means safe, unsafe, confirmed failure, nor severity. Cluster numbers are wording groups. PIADE states are historical events, not causal predictions.

## Scope and evidence

This is a research artifact, not a deployment-ready industrial diagnosis. The source of truth is `README.md`, saved Kaggle reports, and actual code paths. Do not invent outcomes or silently overwrite experimental evidence.
